---
description: Write a handoff note before a break, a branch switch, or a merge
---

Write a short handoff note for my teammate covering:

- What I changed, by file
- What now works that did not before
- What is half-finished, and exactly where I stopped
- Anything that touches `CONTRACTS.md` or the other person's area
- The next single thing someone should pick up

Keep it under 15 lines. This gets pasted into a chat, not filed.
